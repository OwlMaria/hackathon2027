What's CoLi hackathon weekend?

Talking about ideas at a conference is great, but building something together hits different. This hackathon is a chance to step out of the classroom and spend two days actually creating things with other people from the computational linguistics community.

The selection of hackathon tasks will allow for both application-focused and research-focused projects. At the end, teams will share what they've been working on - whether it's a working prototype or research conclusions.

You don't need a specific background to join. Whether you're into development, research, design, or just curious about language and technology, you're welcome here. You can come with a team or find one on the spot and we'll make sure that your work is supported by mentors' feedback.

More details about schedule and logistics are coming soon. This page will be updated as things take shape or, if you don't trust yourself to open it again, join our Newsletter to recieve the updates directly in your inbox! :)
